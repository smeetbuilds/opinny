# Contributor Code of Conduct

Opinny is an open-source project developed by Aahav Labs. We are committed to a respectful, professional and inclusive contributor environment.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, design and decisions rather than people.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing sensitive information.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Follow responsible security disclosure practices.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, spam and knowingly misleading technical or security claims are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to hi@aahavlabs.in. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, restrict participation or take other proportionate action.
